## What the report said

> "The phone number selection pop-up in the field doesn't look good."

That is the whole text of the issue. The scope therefore came from the owner's answer rather than
from the report:

> "The phone number selection pop-up displayed in the field does not look visually appealing. Please
> improve its UI design to make it cleaner, more polished, and consistent with the application's
> existing design system, while preserving its current functionality."

and, separately: **"Use an icon or flag for each country."**

A design ticket of this shape is only reviewable if its claim is measurable, so the diagnosis came
first. The headline is not an opinion: **the popover rendered 208 px narrower than the field it
hangs from**, because a panel-wide `max-width: 320px` silently beat the 528 px the component computed
and wrote inline.

## Blast radius

Unchanged: `src/model/phone/*`, `src/views/grid/AddFieldModal.ts`, `src/views/grid/select/panel.ts`,
`src/views/tableView.ts`, `tablify.schema.json`, `formatVersion`, and the whole write path.

The country block in `styles.css` is shared with the phone cell panel. Every rule added in steps 1–7
is scoped to `.tablify__country-popover`, so the cell panel renders exactly as it did; the two rules
that are shared — the row grid and the flag — are shared **deliberately**, because both hosts render
the same list and leaving one unpolished would fail the "consistent" criterion.
