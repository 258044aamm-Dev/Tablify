# P5-03 — Long-press on mobile (SAD-43)

**Commit:** P5-03 commit (see git log).

## What was built

- `src/views/longPress.ts`: `LongPressDetector`, a pure state machine with injected timers.
  - Touch only. Mouse and pen keep right-click.
  - Fires after **500 ms** of hold (proposed).
  - Cancels when the pointer moves **more than 10 px** from the press start (proposed). Distance is measured from the start, not the last move.
  - Cancels on scroll and on `pointercancel`, so a scroll that starts on a cell does not open the menu.
  - A short tap (released before the threshold) never fires.
- `src/views/tableView.ts` wiring:
  - `pointerdown`/`pointermove`/`pointerup`/`pointercancel` feed the detector. A fired press opens the same menu as right-click (cell or header).
  - `selectstart` is blocked while a touch press is pending or fired (no native selection during the press). `-webkit-touch-callout: none` is set.
  - The click that follows a fired long press is swallowed. The flag resets on every pointer-down, so it never swallows a later click.
  - The native touch context menu is ignored for 1.5 s after a touch, so the menu opens once.
