## What the report said

> "The mobile toolbar supports horizontal swiping, but the current UI does not make this
> interaction obvious to new users. Improve the discoverability of the swipe gesture by adding a
> subtle visual cue…"

Measured gap (plan §2): on phones the tool row scrolls natively and shows exactly 3 tools at a
time (SAD-308); 3 × `calc((100% − 16px)/3)` + 2 × 8 px gaps fill the row **to the pixel**, so
nothing peeks and the interaction is invisible. The fix is a discoverability cue only — no
toolbar behaviour changed.

Owner decisions recorded in the plan: **gradient fade at the scroll edge(s)** · **dynamic
(scroll-aware)** · **mobile only (≤ 639 px)**.

`docs/evidence/sad-375/capture-fades.ts` drives the bundled plugin harness

| mid-scroll | 227 / 453 px | `is-fade-end is-fade-start` | 1 / 1 | `…mid-scroll.png` (both themes) |

The 453 px measured overflow at 390 px confirms the cue is needed and used; the `content`
readings prove the overlays exist on phones (`"" / ""`) and that **nothing paints on desktop**.
Tap-safety is structural: the overlays are `pointer-events: none` (pinned in
`tests/ui/toolbarSwipeCue.test.ts`).

**Deviation note:** the plugin-vs-prototype parity harness compares the `toolbar-row` region
(`tests/visual/lib/regions.ts`) pixel-for-pixel; the prototype has no fade. Like SAD-308's phone
scroll layout before it, the fade is an owner-directed improvement **beyond** the prototype (the
prototype is desktop-oriented). No parity region was edited; this record is the sanctioned
deviation.
