## Deviations and gaps (recorded, not hidden)

1. **Full view does not share the embed's in-memory store.** The spec says the two embeds and the full view share one store. `TableView` (the full view, a `TextFileView`) keeps its own session and was left unchanged on purpose, because changing it risks regressions in existing features. Consistency between the full view and embeds therefore goes through the file: a save by either side fires `modify`, and the other side reloads. An unsaved full-view edit can still be overwritten by a later full-view save (last write wins). **Open: needs a decision and an Obsidian check.**
2. **Undo history is lost on an external reload.** The session is rebuilt from disk.
3. **Embed scope is smaller than the full view.** Context menus, the filter bar, the toolbar, and long-press on mobile are not in the embed. Feature 18 asks for an editable table, and the full view covers the rest.
4. **Timing fixture.** `FX-S` is not in the repo. The 40-row table was generated for the test. The 300 ms target is proposed and was measured only in jsdom.
5. **Prototype match (owner decision Q4).** The embed follows the prototype's structure (fence header, then the grid inside a rounded block). The visual match is **NOT RUN** (no browser or Obsidian runtime in the sandbox).
