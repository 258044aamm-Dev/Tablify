## Re-run tolerance (proposed 10 % — spec rule "otherwise investigate")

Two full runs (`run1`, `run2`): median deltas PERF-1 −18.4 %, PERF-2 +3.3 %, PERF-3 +2.4 %, PERF-5 −10.9 %, PERF-6 +19.5 %, PERF-7 CSV −12.8 % / XLSX +10.1 % / MD +2.2 %. Investigation: shared-container scheduler noise dominates sub-5 ms benchmarks and injects single-sample outliers into p95s (run2 PERF-6 p95 traces to one 54.5 ms sample). All p95s remain ≥ 6× inside their official targets (worst case PERF-1: 166 ms vs 1,000 ms). Conclusion: environmental variance, no code-path change; the authoritative tolerance check is the **second-person re-run on fixed hardware** (owner runbook step "AFTER MEASURING"). No target changed.
