## Re-run tolerance (proposed: within 10 % — investigation notes)

The suite was run twice (`run1`, `run2`). Deltas of the medians: PERF-1 −18.4 %, PERF-2 +3.3 %, PERF-3 +2.4 %, PERF-5 −10.9 %, PERF-6 +19.5 %, PERF-7 CSV −12.8 % / XLSX +10.1 % / MD +2.2 %.

**Investigation (per the spec rule "otherwise investigate"):** the sandbox is a shared container, so single-digit-millisecond benchmarks wobble with scheduler noise; p95s pick up isolated outliers (e.g. run2 PERF-6 p95 54.5 ms driven by one 54.5 ms sample). Benchmarks ≥ 15 ms agree within ±2 ms at the median; every p95 is far inside its official target (worst headroom: PERF-1 at 166 ms vs 1,000 ms = 6× margin). Conclusion: variance is environmental, not a code-path change; **the spec's tolerance check belongs to the second-person re-run on fixed hardware** — recorded as an owner checklist item (runbook, step "AFTER MEASURING"). No target was changed.
