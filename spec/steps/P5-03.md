# P5-03 — Long-press on mobile

- **Linear:** SAD-43
- **Phase:** P5 — Right-click (see `spec/phases/P5.md`)
- **Scope:** MVP
- **Read first:** `spec/glossary.md`, `spec/guidelines.md` §0 (rules) and §1 (assumptions), `spec/roadmap.md` §3 (decisions)
- **Source:** copied unchanged from `spec/guidelines.md` (section for P5-03)

- **Objective:** Long-press opens the same menu as right-click, with no conflict with scroll or selection.
- **Prerequisites:** P5-02.
- **Actions:**
  1. Long-press threshold 500 ms (proposed). Cancel when movement exceeds 10 px (proposed).
  2. Prevent native text selection on the grid while a long-press is detected.
  3. Scroll started on a cell does not open the menu.
- **Dependencies:** P5-02.
- **Outputs:** Touch handler, tests.
- **Verification:**
  - T-M-DEV on iOS and Android: long-press opens menu. Scroll does not. Short tap does not.
  - T-M-DEV: movement during hold cancels the menu.
  - T-U: pointer-event state machine tests with fake timers.
- **Performance:** Long-press detection delay within 50 ms of threshold (proposed).
- **Acceptance:** Device tests pass on both platforms. Results recorded.
